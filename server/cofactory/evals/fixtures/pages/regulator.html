Notice 2026-14: PBM reimbursement transparency reporting

Beginning January 1, 2027, every licensed community pharmacy must retain monthly records reconciling pharmacy benefit manager reimbursements, including any direct and indirect remuneration fees assessed after the point of sale.

Records must be retained for three years and produced to the Board within ten business days of a request. Failure to produce records may result in a fine of up to $1,000 per occurrence.

Questions about this notice may be directed to the Board's compliance office during regular business hours.
